Common questions

What to expect

What do I need to submit before my session?

Depends on the session. For Resume & LinkedIn: your current resume and LinkedIn URL. For Interview Prep: the job description and your resume. For Career Strategy: a brief written summary of your situation and what you're trying to figure out. All materials need to arrive at least 24 hours before your session.

What happens after I pay?

You'll be redirected to a booking page immediately after payment to pick your time. You'll also receive a booking link in your receipt email in case you need it later.

Who do you typically work with?

Tech and engineering professionals at all career stages — from engineers landing their first role to senior leaders navigating promotion or transition. Ideally people who are serious, come prepared, and have a specific outcome in mind.

How is this different from other career coaches?

I've been inside the organizations you want to work in. I've hired thousands of people, managed teams of 50+, and built my own career at Apple, Boeing, Cisco, and Raytheon. I also do the work before we meet — so you're not paying for me to read your resume live.

What is your cancellation policy?

Because I review your materials before we meet, I put in work before we ever sit down together. Reschedule anytime up to 24 hours before your session at no charge. Cancellations within 24 hours are non-refundable, as are no-shows. If something unexpected comes up, reach out and I'll do my best to work with you.
